# Project Scope

## Overview
The Research Funding Innovation Intelligence Platform is designed to simplify the process of discovering research funding opportunities by integrating multiple trusted data sources into a single platform.

## Objectives
- Aggregate research papers, funding opportunities, and patents.
- Provide intelligent search and filtering.
- Recommend relevant funding opportunities based on research interests.
- Support researchers, students, startups, and institutions.

## Functional Scope
- User Registration and Login
- Research Paper Search
- Funding Opportunity Search
- Patent Search
- Personalized Recommendations
- Dashboard and Analytics

## Future Scope
- AI-powered recommendation engine
- NLP-based research similarity detection
- Institution collaboration module
- Funding success prediction
